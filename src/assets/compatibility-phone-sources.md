# Homepage phone card imagery

Twelve original AI-generated, logo-free representative brand-family illustrations were created with Lovable Image Generation on 2026-10-13 for this project. They are not manufacturer photographs, licensed exact-model images, or evidence of a detected model or eSIM compatibility. No third-party product photography was downloaded or used. Manufacturer endorsement and exact-model accuracy are not claimed.

Source: `phone-brands-sheet.png`, a transparent 4-column × 3-row contact sheet. Each phone was cropped separately, trimmed, resized to at most 96 × 180 pixels and encoded as WebP, approximately 1.7–3.2 KB each.

Order: Apple / Samsung / Google / Xiaomi; POCO / Redmi / Oppo / OnePlus; Motorola / Honor / Huawei / Nothing. Runtime assets: `compatibility-{brand}.webp`.

The existing phone trio was not reused because it cannot match one selected brand. No existing verified, permitted exact-model photographs were identified. Unknown/unsupported brands and failed images use the general phone icon. Exact-model selections retain the representative brand image until a verified and permitted exact-model asset is available.
